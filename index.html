Music Bot Overview

Welcome to Music Bot! A Discord bot designed to play high-quality music in voice channels via YouTube and Spotify searches, featuring dedicated request channel support and interactive controls.

Key Features

  • Dedicated Request Channels: Automatically process song links or titles typed into designated music request channels.
  • YouTube & Spotify Integration: Search and stream audio seamlessly using yt-dlp and Spotify metadata resolution.
  • Interactive UI Controls: Control music playback (Pause/Resume, Skip, Stop) with interactive buttons directly on the now-playing embed.
  • Real-Time Queue Display: Dynamic queue messages that update instantly as new tracks are requested or skipped.
  • Auto-Disconnect & Inactivity Timers: Automatically leaves voice channels when empty or inactive for 5 minutes to optimize resources.
  • Volume Adjustment: Smooth volume controls scaling from 1% to 100%.

Bot Commands

  • /음악채널 : Designate current channel or create a new dedicated text channel for music requests.
  • /볼륨 [1-100] : Adjust the playback volume for your server.
  • /스킵 : Skip the currently playing track.
  • /강제종료 : Stop playback, clear the queue, and leave the voice channel.
